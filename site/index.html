How we work

From the first training to the last wave

A big-wave session lasts a few hours. Getting ready for it takes months. Here is how the team works, step by step.

Rides the wave. Trains all year to handle its size and speed.

Surfer

Drives the Jet Ski and pulls the surfer into the wave at the right moment and speed.

Pilot

Watches the ocean from the cliff and tells the team by radio which waves are coming.

Spotter

Waits nearby on a second Jet Ski, ready to pick up the surfer after a fall.

Rescue
  1. 01

    Prepare

    Fitness, breath-hold practice and medical checks on land, all year round.

  2. 02

    Learn and train together

    Surfers and pilots practise pick-ups, hand signals and tow‑ins together in calm water, long before the big days.

  3. 03

    Check the equipment

    Boards, life vests, radios, Jet Skis and rescue sleds are checked and repaired before every swell.

  4. 04

    Plan the session

    The team reads the forecast, agrees who does what and goes through the safety plan together.

  5. 05

    Work on the water

    Surfers ride, pilots tow them in, the spotter guides from the cliff and the rescue team stays ready.

  6. 06

    Review

    After the session the team watches the footage and talks through what worked and what to change. This talk is called a debrief.

The kit
Jet Ski

A small, fast water motorbike. The team uses it to pull surfers into waves too fast to catch by paddling, and to get them out of danger.

Used by
  • Pilot
  • Rescue
Rescue sled

A soft, flat platform fixed to the back of the Jet Ski. After a fall the surfer grabs it and lies on it, and the pilot drives them away from the breaking waves.

Used by
  • Surfer
  • Rescue

Nazaré Experience

Spend time with TAKE HIGH in Nazaré. Meet the team, get a safety briefing, see the equipment and how the crew prepares, and get closer to the world of big waves than the shore allows.

If the weather, the ocean and safety allow, it can include the lighthouse, the route between the cliffs, small or big waves, a Jet Ski ride or the rescue sled. Nothing on the water is guaranteed; we agree the plan with you before the day.
